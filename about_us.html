Who We Are

Our Story

Anatomy; A opened as a two-bay workshop on the edge of the industrial belt, run by a father-and-daughter team who'd both trained as qualified panel beaters before deciding to run things their own way.

Seventeen years on, the workshop has grown to six bays, a dedicated spray booth, and a computerised colour-matching lab — but the standard hasn't moved. Every car that leaves our gate is measured, road-tested, and signed off by the technician who worked it.

We work directly with all major insurers, so a claim doesn't mean handing your car to a stranger. You still deal with the same team, from assessment to handover.

Our Guarantee

Every repair carries a 12-month workmanship guarantee. If a repaired panel or paint finish fails because of our work, we fix it. See our services for what each job covers.

How We Work

Every job starts on the measuring bench, not the tool rack. We map the damage against factory chassis specs before a single panel is pulled.

Our spectrophotometer reads your original paint's exact formula, including fade from age and sun, so the new panel matches the car in front of your house.

A straight-looking panel over a bent chassis is a liability. Every structural repair is jig-checked and road-tested before we call a job finished.
