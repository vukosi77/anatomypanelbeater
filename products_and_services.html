How a Job Moves Through the Shop

Every vehicle follows the same six stages, in the same order, whether it's a dent or a full rebuild.

  1. Assess — Free damage assessment and written quote.
  2. Strip — Damaged panels removed, chassis measured.
  3. Repair — Panel reshaping or structural straightening.
  4. Paint — Colour-matched spray and clear-coat cure.
  5. Check — Quality inspection and road test.
  6. Handover — Wash, walkthrough, and keys back to you.
